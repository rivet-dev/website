title: "Company-Specific Agents"
description: "Run agents against internal data, inside your own network, on Rivet Actors."
---

A full guide is on its way. In the meantime, start from the [AI Agent example](https://github.com/rivet-dev/actors/tree/main/examples/ai-agent): queue-driven Actor agents with streaming Vercel AI SDK responses, one Actor per agent. To keep the agents and their data inside your network, deploy with [Bring Your Own Cloud](/docs/deploy/byoc/) or [self-host](/docs/deploy/self-host/control-plane/).
