## Deployment Options

Nodes always run your code, so you always own them. The options differ in where the control plane and storage run, and who operates them.

| | Control plane and storage | Nodes | Operated by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Rivet Cloud & managed nodes** | Rivet Cloud | Rivet Cloud | Rivet |
| **Rivet Cloud & bring your own nodes** | Rivet Cloud | Your infrastructure | Rivet |
| **Bring your own cloud** | Your VPC | Your infrastructure | Rivet |
| **Self-host** | Your infrastructure | Your infrastructure | You |

### Rivet Cloud & managed nodes

Rivet Cloud runs the control plane, storage, and your [actor nodes](/docs/deploy/cloud/compute). There is nothing to operate.

### Bring your own cloud

Rivet Enterprise Edition runs inside your VPC, operated by Rivet. An operator in your cluster pulls deployment instructions from Rivet Cloud and maintains the deployment. Your nodes run alongside it.

## Architecture

Rivet has 3 core components:

- **Node**: The process running your Rivet Actors, with the Rivet SDK. It connects outbound to the control plane, so it never needs a public URL or an inbound firewall rule.
- **Control plane**: Schedules Rivet Actors onto nodes, routes requests to them, and serves the API.
- **Storage**: An object storage bucket that holds each Rivet Actor's durable data. The control plane replicates writes across its own disks first.

See [Architecture](/docs/architecture) for how they fit together.
