BYOC deploys Rivet Enterprise Edition and FoundationDB inside your VPC, fully managed by Rivet.

- **Managed by Rivet**: We handle deployment updates and maintenance
- **No inbound management connection**: The operator connects to Rivet Cloud
- **Public or private access**: Expose Rivet over HTTPS or keep it accessible only through your private network
- **Dashboard included**: Inspect and manage your actors
- **Choose where workers run**: Kubernetes, ECS, Cloud Run, and more

## Get started

- [Quickstart](/cloud/byoc/quickstart): download your setup kit, provision infrastructure, and connect your first worker.
- [Architecture](/cloud/byoc/architecture): understand the operator, permissions, and network options.
